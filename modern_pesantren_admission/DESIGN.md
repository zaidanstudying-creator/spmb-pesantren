## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an **Islamic Modern & Institutional Elegance** aesthetic tailored for an admission and registration portal (SPMB/e-PPDB). It balances academic rigor, spiritual serenity, and technological sophistication. 

### Brand Personality & Emotional Core
- **Trustworthy & Authoritative:** Instills immediate confidence in parents, prospective students, and administrative boards through structured clarity, balanced hierarchy, and formal visual poise.
- **Serene & Grounded:** Inspired by classical Islamic values and architectural balance, utilizing dignified deep emerald tones paired with noble gold and quiet slate neutrals.
- **Contemporary & Approachable:** Eliminates friction in bureaucratic registration through intuitive digital flows, generous breathing space, and accessible ergonomics.

### Design Principles
1. **Institutional Dignity:** Refined typography pairings that celebrate prestige without leaning into archaic ornament. 
2. **Form Clarity:** Registration steps, document uploads, and verification tracks must be effortless, unambiguous, and reassuring at every step.
3. **Structured Restraint:** Shadows are muted and natural; borders are delicate and purposeful; accent colors (such as amber gold) are reserved exclusively for critical alerts, honors, or key milestones.

## Colors

The palette establishes an institutional identity that feels rooted, intellectual, and welcoming. 

### Role Mapping & Semantic Tokens
- **Primary (`#065f46` - Deep Emerald):** The foundational brand color representing integrity, spiritual heritage, and academic authority. Used for header bars, primary interactive buttons, active navigation states, and strong brand anchors. Supporting shade `#047857` functions as the active/hover state.
- **Secondary (`#d97706` - Warm Amber / Antique Gold):** Used sparingly as an accent for prestige accents, fee payment callouts, pending status indicators, and ceremonial highlights. Accompanied by `#f59e0b` for hover interactions.
- **Tertiary (`#10b981` - Sage / Mint Emerald):** Signals validation, completed steps in steppers, verified document tags, and positive system states.
- **Neutral Surface & Canvas:** 
  - Canvas base: `#f8fafc` (Slate 50) and `#f1f5f9` (Slate 100) provide soft, anti-glare canvas tones.
  - Surface layers: `#ffffff` for elevated cards, forms, and data grids.
  - Borders & Dividers: `#e2e8f0` (Slate 200) ensuring crisp, razor-thin structural definition.
- **Neutral Content (`#0f172a` - Deep Slate Navy):** Provides high-contrast typography, sharp iconography, and solemn footer/navigation structures, paired with `#475569` (Slate 600) for body and secondary meta labels.
- **System Semantic Badges:**
  - *Success (Approved/Lolos):* Emerald (`#065f46` text on `#d1fae5` surface).
  - *Warning (Verification Pending):* Amber (`#92400e` text on `#fef3c7` surface).
  - *Critical (Incomplete/Revision Required):* Rose (`#9f1239` text on `#ffe4e6` surface).
  - *Info (Announcement/Scheduled Test):* Sky (`#075985` text on `#e0f2fe` surface).

## Typography

The typography unites traditional academic prestige with functional digital legibility.

- **Display & Headings (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for institutional mastheads, welcome banners, formal admission decrees, and major section titles (`headline-xl` through `headline-md`). It conveys historic authenticity, intellectual depth, and honor.
- **Interface & Operational Text (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Drives all functional levels from `headline-sm`, `title`, down to `label-sm`. Its generous x-height, open apertures, and crisp geometry ensure high legibility in forms, candidate tables, and mobile viewports.
- **Hierarchy Rules:**
  - Editorial headings (`headline-xl`, `headline-lg`) are styled in dark slate (`#0f172a`) or deep emerald (`#065f46`).
  - Form field labels always use `label-md` with strict medium-to-semibold weights to ensure rapid visual scanning by parents and guardians.
  - Numbers, registration codes (e.g., `REG-2025-0811`), and currency values use tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) within `Plus Jakarta Sans`.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored on a rigid 8-point spatial system embedded within a responsive 12-column grid.

### Layout System
- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12 columns, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters, `2rem` (32px) exterior margins, with an optimal central container capped at `1280px` for portal views and `840px` for multi-step registration forms to maintain ergonomic reading line-lengths.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, `1.25rem` (20px) gutters, `1.5rem` (24px) exterior margins.
- **Mobile (<= 767px):** 4 columns, `1rem` (16px) gutters, `1rem` (16px) canvas margins. Form controls expand to full width (single column flow).

### Spacing Application
- `space-xs` (4px): Used between iconography and adjacent button labels or micro-indicators.
- `space-sm` (8px): Used between form labels and input fields, or between badge items.
- `space-md` (16px): Internal padding for standard table cells, small cards, and stepper node margins.
- `space-lg` (24px): Card interior padding, metric box interior spacing, and form section gaps.
- `space-xl` (40px): Gaps between separate major modular blocks on administrative dashboards and registration stages.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined, architectural surface layering system rather than heavy drop shadows.

### Surface Tiers & Depth Hierarchy
1. **Base Tier (Canvas):** Colored `#f8fafc`. Background canvas for all views.
2. **Layer 1 (Cards, Data Tables, Forms):** Pure white `#ffffff` resting directly on the canvas, bounded by a crisp 1px stroke of `#e2e8f0` (Slate 200) and an ultra-subtle ambient lift: `0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
3. **Layer 2 (Hovered Cards, Dropdowns, Datepickers):** Elevated elements receive a warm-tinted emerald-slate drop: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(6, 95, 70, 0.04), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`, coupled with border refinement `#cbd5e1`.
4. **Layer 3 (Modals, Slide-over Previewers, Drawers):** `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`. Backdrops use `#0f172a` with a 45% alpha channel and `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`.

### Border Integrity
Crisp borders take precedence over extreme elevation. Cards, inputs, and tab containers maintain an explicit hairline border to ensure structural stability on varying screen calibrations.

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Balanced Rounded** shape language (`roundedness: 2` = base 8px / 0.5rem radius), combining modern digital approachability with institutional rectitude.

### Corner Radius Standards
- **Base Components (`rounded`, 8px):** Buttons, text input boxes, dropdown select menus, and file upload zones.
- **Large Containers (`rounded-lg`, 16px):** Form milestone cards, dashboard metric cards, and responsive data tables.
- **Overlays (`rounded-xl`, 24px):** Admission confirmation modals, document preview sheets, and verification popovers.
- **Pill Shape (`rounded-full`):** Exclusively reserved for status badges, step number indicators, and avatar chips. This contrast between crisp rectangular cards and soft circular tags makes status recognition instant.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#065f46`, text `#ffffff`, border none, 8px radius. Hover: `#047857` with slight vertical translate (`-1px`). Active: `#064e3b`.
- **Secondary / Institutional:** Background `#ffffff`, text `#065f46`, border `1.5px solid #065f46`. Hover: background `#ecfdf5`.
- **Amber Action (Payment/Special CTA):** Background `#d97706`, text `#ffffff`. Hover: `#b45309`.
- **Sizes:** Small (36px height, `label-md`), Medium (44px height, `label-lg`), Large (52px height for final submission, `title-sm`).

### 2. Status Badges & Chips
- Designed as pill shapes (`rounded-full`), `label-sm`, padding `4px 12px`, with soft pastel backgrounds and strong tonal text:
  - *Verified / Lolos:* `#ecfdf5` background, `#065f46` text, hairline `#a7f3d0` border.
  - *Pending / Menunggu Pembayaran:* `#fffbeb` background, `#b45309` text, hairline `#fde68a` border.
  - *Revision / Berkas Ditolak:* `#fff1f2` background, `#be123c` text, hairline `#fecdd3` border.
  - *Test Scheduled:* `#f0f9ff` background, `#0369a1` text, hairline `#bae6fd` border.

### 3. Multi-Step Form Stepper (e-PPDB Track)
- Horizontal bar on desktop, collapsible sticky bar on mobile.
- **Nodes:** 32px circular indicators.
  - *Completed:* `#065f46` fill with an interior white checkmark icon. Connected by a 2px `#065f46` solid line.
  - *Active:* `#065f46` border (2px), white fill, containing an inner 10px `#065f46` solid dot. Text is `label-lg` in `#065f46`.
  - *Upcoming:* `#e2e8f0` border, white fill, slate text `#94a3b8`. Connected by a dashed `#e2e8f0` line.

### 4. Input Fields & File Uploaders
- **Text Inputs:** 44px height, background `#ffffff`, border `1px solid #cbd5e1`, 8px radius, text `body-md` in `#0f172a`. Focus state: border `#065f46` with an outer ring glow of `0 0 0 3px rgba(6, 95, 70, 0.12)`.
- **File Upload Zones (Ijazah / KK / Akta):** Dashed border `2px dashed #cbd5e1`, hover border `#065f46`, background `#f8fafc`. Includes an emerald document icon, clear file format instructions, and a file size badge. Once uploaded, it converts to a solid white card with a mini preview and green verification checkmark.

### 5. Data Tables & Action Bars
- **Container:** Pure white card with `#e2e8f0` border, 16px radius, overflow hidden.
- **Header:** Background `#f8fafc`, text uppercase `label-sm` in `#475569`, border bottom `1px solid #e2e8f0`.
- **Row:** Height 56px, border bottom `1px solid #f1f5f9`, hover transition to `#f8fafc`.
- **Integrated Action Bar:** Houses filter drop-downs (Gelombang Pendaftaran, Jenjang, Status), real-time search input, and batch export buttons.

### 6. Metric Counters
- Used in administrative dashboards and portal overview screens.
- Layout: White card with an accent top-border or icon container tinted in deep emerald or gold.
- Value typography: `32px` semibold tabular figures in `#0f172a`.
- Caption: `label-md` in `#64748b` paired with a directional trend pill badge.

### 7. Modal Document Previewers
- Centered dialog with a dark translucent backdrop (`#0f172a` at 45% opacity with 4px blur).
- Fixed header with candidate identifier, Playfair Display title, and a dual-action toolbar (Download PDF, Approve, Request Re-upload).
- Document canvas area styled with light-neutral matte background to maintain document contrast.
